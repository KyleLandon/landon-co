---
name: Homepage brand positioning
description: Business-first tone and scope for public studio copy
---

Homepage messaging should emphasize business outcomes, credibility, efficiency, and long-term partnership. Keep the tone straightforward, modern, and founder-led; do not imply a large corporate agency or position the studio as the cheapest option.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested accessible, practical positioning without “affordable” or “without breaking the bank” language.

**How to apply:** Lead service descriptions with customer benefits, keeping technical names such as React and TypeScript in capabilities. Preserve the existing design when updating copy. Website Care includes small content changes, not unlimited redesign or larger development requests.
